# October Poem for poidh — Ten Stanzas of Proof (`base#1463`)

**Author:** `@umpireboulianne-design` (`0x2C306283E41Fea31cE0b42003467fF5658E8Ced1`)  
**Human Editing & Structure Note:** Original 10-stanza poem structured chronologically from Month I (January) to Month X (October = 10th month), four lines per stanza in AABB/ABAB meter, edited and typeset onto a custom broadsheet proof image for `@poidhxyz`.

![October Poem Broadsheet](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/umpireboulianne-design/poidh-proofs/main/october_poidh_poem.png)

---

### I. January — Genesis of Intent
A whispered wish in winter's frost takes nothing from the air,  
Until a stake is locked in code to prove the will is there;  
On poidh the idle promise dies, replaced by vaulted gold,  
Where every bounty names the deed before the tale is told.

### II. February — The Escrow Covenant
No middleman holds back the key, no gatekeeper delays,  
The smart contract on Base awaits the proof that action pays;  
A solo dare or open pool where strangers join the quest,  
And escrowed ETH stands sentinel to put the claim to test.

### III. March — Pics or It Didn't Happen
Speak not of what you meant to build or mountains nearly climbed,  
Show us the frame, the hash, the lens, the moment captured, timed;  
For "pics or it didn't happen" is the law that clears the noise,  
And turns a thousand empty boasts to verifiable poise.

### IV. April — The Hunter's Craft
From street-side chalk to lines of Rust, from comics drawn at night,  
Each claimant mints an artifact and brings the work to light;  
An IPFS permalink, a timestamped frame of truth,  
Where human hands and agent minds both furnish living proof.

### V. May — Multiplayer Coordination
One spark becomes a commonwealth when backers pool their weight,  
Each wei contributed bestows a voice to arbitrate;  
No boardroom vote, no paper form, just skin inside the game,  
Where funders judge the evidence and crown the winning claim.

### VI. June — The Meridian of Trust
In summer's glare the ledger shines on Base and Arbitrum,  
Where every bounty's history is open to the sum;  
Two point five percent the protocol, the rest flows clean and fast,  
From idea to execution when the final vote is cast.

### VII. July — Freedom in the Field
Touch grass, plant trees, debug a node, or paint a public wall,  
The screen is just the clearinghouse that answers every call;  
Because the world outside the chain is where the bounty lives,  
And proof is how the physical receives what crypto gives.

### VIII. August — The Sovereign Ledger
No resume, no border check, no permission to apply,  
Your wallet and your work alone are all the reason why;  
An open market forged in truth where anyone can start,  
And turn a bold bounty prompt into a paid-out work of art.

### IX. September — The Harvest of Proofs
As autumn gathers what was sown across the social feed,  
Each settled NFT remains a monument to deed;  
A gallery of finished tasks where skeptics once stood still,  
Proving that open bounties bend the future to our will.

### X. October — The Tenth Month's Seal
Now Tenth-month lanterns light the dark and ten full stanzas close,  
On poidh.xyz the bounty lives where proved action grows;  
From thought to stake, from stake to proof, from proof to instant pay:  
Pics or it didn't happen — show your work and claim the day.
